## Company Overview

ROOTYM is an Indian agricultural export company focused on connecting trusted Indian agricultural products with global buyers.

The company works to provide quality-focused sourcing, transparent communication, reliable export processes, and long-term partnerships with international customers.

ROOTYM's vision is to become one of the world's most trusted AI-powered digital export companies for Indian agricultural products.
